## Form: "What did you build?" (draft, 20-2000 chars)
"Coop Tycoon is a gamified financial-literacy game for kids. Players run a tiny egg business: buy hens, keep the coop clean, handle feed-price spikes and sickness, and sell eggs to an NPC buyer that has its own wallet and whose price moves with supply and demand. A rule-based AI mentor explains every gain and loss. Big purchases need approval from a guardian through a Sputnik DAO proposal, and a class savings goal mints a bonus of $COOP (NEP-141, testnet) to every member when reached. The economy is the gameplay and players never see wallets or gas. Runs in mock mode with zero setup, and in live mode on NEAR testnet with verifiable transactions in an in-app Proof drawer. Virtual tokens only; no real money and no gambling mechanics."
